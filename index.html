Every make and model on UK roads, brought to life

Race the top makes through time, drill from make to model to variant, profile any manufacturer and compare models side by side. Headline figures are built in; load the official make and model files from GOV.UK to unlock the full explorer.

How it works

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you load is uploaded.

  1. 1Press Get latest data. The page finds the newest release on GOV.UK and downloads it.
  2. 2New registrations appear first; licensed vehicles follow in the background.
  3. 3Next time, the data opens instantly from this browser, and you'll be told when GOV.UK publishes an update.

How fuel types are grouped
Battery electric and fuel cell count as zero emission; plug-in hybrid and range extended as plug-in hybrid; other hybrids as hybrid; then petrol, diesel, and everything else as other. The original fuel values are kept and shown in tooltips and the table.

        Location explorer

        Where vehicles are kept

        Uses the small-area files: plug-in vehicles (VEH0145), ultra low emission vehicles (VEH0135) and all licensed vehicles (VEH0125) for every small area (LSOA) in the UK.

        Make and model are not available by area. The published files don't break makes or models down by location, so these views cover all vehicles by body type and keepership, and plug-in / ultra low emission vehicles by fuel type.

        Vehicles are placed at the registered keeper's postcode. Company vehicles appear at the company's registered address, so big lease and rental fleets show up as spikes where the company is registered, and can jump when a company moves. Use “Private keepers only” for a truer picture of where people live.

        Small areas that never had 5 or more vehicles, vehicles between keepers and invalid postcodes are combined in a “Miscellaneous” row: it is included in totals but left off maps and rankings.

          The explorer uses the official make and model files published on GOV.UK. They are downloaded straight into this page and never uploaded anywhere.

          Coverage
            Other ways to load the data

            1. Paste a direct file link

            On the GOV.UK data files page, right-click a file name such as df_VEH0160_UK.csv, choose Copy link, then paste it here. You can paste several links at once, one per line. You can also paste the address of the GOV.UK page itself.

            2. Download the files, then drop them here

            Download df_VEH0160_UK.csv (new registrations) and df_VEH0120_UK.csv (licensed vehicles) from GOV.UK vehicle licensing statistics data files, then drag them onto this box.
